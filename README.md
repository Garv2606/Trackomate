# Trackomate — Your Personal Budgeting App

A student-focused budgeting app designed to fix the "boom-and-bust" monthly spending cycle college students face.

**Team:** Fincom — IIT Ropar
**Members:** Garv Raj, Abhishek Aggarwal

## Problem

Students go from cash-rich (days 1–10) to cash-broke (days 11–30) every month due to sudden financial autonomy without budgeting frameworks. Core pain points:

- **No visibility** — small daily spends (chai, autos) add up unnoticed
- **Constant anxiety** — fear of running out of money before month-end
- **Paralyzing complexity** — existing finance apps are built for salaried professionals
- **Unachievable goals** — no simple way to save for a trip, gadget, or emergency fund

## User Research

Four personas guided the design: **Aarav** (anxious achiever), **Priya** (resourceful freelancer), **Rohan** (social spender), **Ishaan** (forgetful fragmenter). Key findings — peer-pressure spending, low financial literacy, lack of student-aligned tools, and irregular cash flows — pointed to a need for a behavior-first, education-in-context, practically flexible product.

## MVP (RICE-Prioritized)

1. **Manual Expense Tracking** — log cash/UPI spend with student-friendly categories
2. **Smart Budgeting** — auto-suggested 50/30/20 split from monthly allowance
3. **Savings Pots** — visual, goal-based savings (trip, gadget, etc.)
4. **Simple Dashboard** — clean view of spend, budget, and savings progress
5. **Contextual Nudges** — bite-sized financial tips triggered by real spending behavior

*Deliberately excluded from MVP:* SMS sync, bill splitting, investment integration, credit score monitoring — valuable but high-effort, non-essential for v1.

## Go-To-Market Strategy

1. **On-Ground Army** — Campus Ambassador program driving peer-led adoption
2. **Digital Buzz** — authentic Instagram/WhatsApp presence (Reels, polls, trivia)
3. **Launch Week Blitz** — branded stalls, "Financial Fitness Challenge," referral drive
4. **Scale & Sustain** — data-driven refinement, expansion to more colleges

Target: 1,000 users in the first week across 3 pilot colleges.

## Roadmap

- **Months 1–2 (Launch & Stabilize):** Deploy MVP, <1% crash rate, 20+ user interviews
- **Months 1–2 (Deepen Engagement):** Custom categories, quick-add widget, gamified savings
- **Months 1–2 (Retention Driver):** Group budgeting, viral growth loops, backend scaling

## Success Metrics

- **North Star Metric:** Weekly Active Budgeters (3+ transactions logged + budget check within 7 days)
- **Week 4 Retention:** >20%
- **Activation Rate:** >75% (onboarding + 3 expenses logged in 24 hrs)
- **DAU/MAU Stickiness:** >30%
- **Community Savings Momentum:** 95% MoM growth in total Savings Pot value
